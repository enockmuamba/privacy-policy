Privacy Policy

Introduction

oberpage ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or use the oberpage app. We believe in a clean, distraction-free experience with no mandatory user accounts.

Information We Do Not Collect

oberpage does not require you to register or create an account. We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the core feed.

Local Storage (Your Collections)

When you use the "Save" feature to bookmark capsules into your collection, this data is stored locally on your own device using browser Local Storage. We do not host, store, or have access to your saved personal collections on any remote server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to monitor general traffic trends and application performance. Google Analytics may collect anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time spent on the app, and basic device properties. This data is entirely aggregated and anonymous, used solely to understand which capsules get the most engagement so we can improve the content experience.

You can learn more about how Google handles data here: Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics may use cookies to gather standard internet log information and visitor behavior data. You can easily disable or block cookies through your individual browser settings if you prefer not to allow them.
